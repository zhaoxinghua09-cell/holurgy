**EN**
Holurgy is the engineering of wholeness: integrating factors into structures from which emergence is explained. It shares the synthesis family with Synthesurgy but emphasizes holistic argumentation — why the whole governs the behavior of the parts.

整体工程术是整体大于部分之和的整合方法：因素→结构→涌现。与 Synthesurgy 同族，侧重整体性论证。
